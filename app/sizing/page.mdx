# Responsive sizing

<Lead>By default an interface draws at the exact `width` / `height` you give it, in pixels (600×400 if unset). The `responsive` option makes it fit its parent instead. `"scale"` stretches one drawing to the container through a **viewBox** and keeps the aspect ratio. `"reflow"` **redraws at native pixels** on resize: text stays crisp, the width tracks the container, and the height stays fixed. `responsive: true` is the same as `"reflow"`. In every mode, the `width` / `height` you pass is the design size.</Lead>

<ApiReference entries={api} />

<Section id="scale" title="Scale (viewBox)">

`responsive: "scale"` keeps the chart's 480×300 aspect ratio and stretches it to fill the column. Text and strokes scale with it. Use it when a chart should grow and shrink with its container, and text and strokes can scale too.

<Section id="reflow" title="Reflow (ResizeObserver)">

`responsive: "reflow"` redraws at native pixels to fit this card. The width tracks the container and the height stays 300. Resize the window and the bars lay out again at full sharpness. Call `el.destroy()` on unmount.
