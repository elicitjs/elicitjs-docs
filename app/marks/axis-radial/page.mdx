# Radial axis

<Lead>`axisRadial()` draws the `theta` scale as a **radial axis**: an arc spine, ticks, labels, and optional coloured bands for categories. `orient` defaults to `top`, running left to right through the top. `channel: 'radius'` draws the radius scale as concentric rings instead. The axis is inert; pair it with a `needle` to take an answer.</Lead>

<Section id="continuous" title="Continuous radial ticks">

A quantitative `theta` scale gets a spine and tick marks. `theta` is the polar angular position, which is what a needle encodes. `angle`, a mark's rotation in place, has no axis.

<Section id="ticks" title="Tick + label customization">

Ticks take the same options as Cartesian axes: `ticks` (a count), `tickValues` (explicit values), and `tickFormat` (a d3-format string or function). Style labels with `fontSize` and `labelFill`, and the spine with `stroke`.

<Section id="radius" title="Rings for a radius scale">

`axisRadial({ channel: 'radius' })` reads the `radius` scale instead of `theta`. It draws a concentric ring at each tick, labelled up the vertical. Use it to key an [arc](/marks/arc#radius) whose slices take their rim from a `radius` field. The rings span the scale's own extent, from the arc's `innerRadius` to its `outerRadius`, so the axis's `radius` option has no effect. `ticks`, `tickValues` and `tickFormat` work as on the angular axis.

</Section>
