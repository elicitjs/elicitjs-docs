# Symbol & Emoji

<Lead>The `symbol` channel maps a category to a **glyph**, an emoji or a unicode shape, as `fill` maps a category to a colour. It is a channel, not a mark: `point`, `dotStack` and `waffle` draw the glyph in place of their circle or rect, and [`shape`](/marks/shape) reads it as a named shape or SVG path. The elicited value is the category. Edit it with `cycle()` or `edit.legend.category()`.</Lead>

<ApiReference entries={api} />

<Section id="point" title="Emoji points — click to cycle">

Here the `y` position and the glyph both encode `mood`. A `cycle()` on the `symbol` channel advances the category on click. The face changes and the point moves to its new row, since both channels read the same field.

<Example
  code={moodOverTheWeek}
  title="Mood over the week"
  blurb="symbol maps the ordinal mood to a face; cycle() advances it on click."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Click</b> a face to cycle its mood.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="constant" title="One fixed glyph — no mapping">

The `symbol: '⚽'` shorthand draws one glyph on every point. It desugars to a constant channel and skips the scale, as `fill: 'red'` does. Here the elicited value is `goals`: drag a ball up or down to set that stage's total.

<Example
  code={worldCupGoals}
  title="World Cup goals"
  blurb="A constant symbol: '⚽' — one glyph for every point, no scale. The elicited value is goals; drag a ball up or down to set it."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Drag</b> a ⚽ up or down to change that stage's goals.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="tokens" title="Emoji tokens — drop and remove">

A constant `symbol` shorthand makes every token the same glyph. `create` and `remove` work as on plain dots, here as a ⭐ star-rating counter.

<Example
  code={starTokens}
  title="Star tokens"
  blurb="dotStack of ⭐ tokens; click a slot to add, click a star to remove."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Click</b> a column to add a ⭐, <b>click</b> a ⭐ to remove it.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="waffle" title="Emoji waffle — count in glyphs">

A `symbol` channel draws every waffle cell as its category's glyph, so you count a quantity in 🍎. Drag to fill. Empty cells draw faint and stay grabbable.

<Example
  code={fruitBasket}
  title="Fruit basket"
  blurb="Each category's cells render its emoji; edit.waffle.fill drags the count."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Drag</b> up a column to change its count.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="legend" title="A glyph legend / picker">

`legendSymbol({ edit: edit.legend.category() })` draws a row of glyphs you click to set the category. It is the same [legend](/marks/legend) as a colour legend, drawn with glyphs instead of swatches.

</Section>
