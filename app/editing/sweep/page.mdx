# Mark-specific & path edits

<Lead>Certain marks maintain multi-row or geometric invariants that require specialised edits. `edit.line.sweep()` repaints points across columns, `edit.line.anchor()` builds connected paths, and `edit.stack.edge()` and `edit.stack.cut()` reallocate slices in pies and stacked bars.</Lead>

<ApiReference entries={api} />

<Section id="sweep" title="Line sweep (you-draw-it)">

`edit.line.sweep()` updates each point's value as the pointer crosses its horizontal column — the canonical "you-draw-it" interaction. The $x$ coordinates remain fixed while the curve conforms to the gesture.

<Example
  code={drawACurve}
  title="Sweep to shape a curve"
  blurb={"edit: move({ pick: \"sweep\" }) on the y channel paints values as you drag horizontally."}
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Sweep</b> left-to-right across the chart.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="series" title="Series-scoped multi-line sweeps">

When multiple lines share a plot, a sweep locks onto the nearest line at drag-start and paints only that series. Other series remain untouched.

<Example
  code={twoLinesSweptIndependently}
  title="Two lines swept independently"
  blurb="The sweep locks onto the closest series at the start of the drag."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Sweep</b> over one line to reshape it; the other remains fixed.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="anchors" title="Line anchors & freehand paths">

`edit.line.anchor()` appends new points to the nearest line or starts a fresh series in empty space. `edit.line.draw()` samples pointer movement to record freehand continuous strokes.

<Example
  code={clickToAddAnAnchor}
  title="Click to add an anchor"
  blurb="Clicking empty space appends the next anchor point to the line."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Drag</b> an existing point, or <b>click</b> empty space to append an anchor.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="stacks-and-arcs" title="Arc slices & stacked bars">

Stacked marks (such as `pie`, `donut`, and `barY`) use `edit.stack.edge()` and `edit.stack.cut()` to modify proportions while holding the total constant.

`edit.stack.edge()` lets you drag interior seams between segments. `edit.stack.cut()` clicks inside a segment to split it into distinct categories drawn from the schema domain.

<Example
  code={cutAStack}
  title="Cut a stack into categories"
  blurb="Click inside a 100% bar to cut it into categories; drag seams to adjust proportions."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Click</b> inside the bar to cut it into asset categories · <b>drag</b> seams to rebalance.
    </>
  )}
/>

<Example

</Section>
