# Editing

<Lead>An edit writes a gesture back to the data, through the same scale that encodes it. It sets a `gesture` (`drag`, `click`, `dblclick`, `commit` or `wheel`), a `pick` (`direct`, `nearest`, `plane`, `probe` or `sweep`), and optionally `when`, `constrain` and `guide`; its `apply` performs the inversion. Declare it on a channel (`channels.y.edit`), at mark level (`edits: [...]`), or on a [legend](/editing/legend) or [axis](/editing/axis).</Lead>

<ApiReference entries={api} />

<Section id="move" title="Move a value">

`move()` inverts the pointer on each positional channel it governs. On a bar's `y`, a drag rewrites the value.

<Section id="pick" title="Pick — how an edit finds its target">

`pick: "direct"` uses the mark under the pointer. `pick: "nearest"` takes the closest mark within a pixel threshold, so you can grab small marks from nearby empty space. `guide: true` draws the snap ring.
