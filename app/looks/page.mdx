# Looks

<Lead>A **look** changes how the finished chart is drawn: by hand with `looks.rough()`, or through a shader with `looks.sketch()`, `looks.cartoon()`, `looks.halftone()` and `looks.custom()`. Set them in `looks: [...]` on the `Elicit` spec. A look changes no data, scale or gesture. Looks are experimental, so their names and options may change.</Lead>

<ApiReference entries={api} />

<Section id="rough" title="Drawn by hand">

`looks.rough()` redraws each shape with a pen that wobbles. Lines are drawn twice, corners overshoot, and a mark's fill is hatched. It draws in both [renderers](/renderers).

Each shape keeps its true outline for the pointer, so a drag grabs a bar where it sits. `roughness` sets how far the pen strays, from 0 to 3. `fill: "solid"` fills a mark with colour in place of hatching. `seed` picks another hand, and the same seed draws the same chart every time.

<Example
  code={roughBars}
  title="A hand-drawn bar chart"
  blurb="looks: [looks.rough()]. The bars are hatched and their edges wobble; the drag still reads each bar's true shape."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Drag</b> a bar up or down.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="scope" title="What a look covers">

`scope` is `"marks"` by default: the look covers the marks that draw data, and axes, labels, legends and guides stay as drawn. `scope: "frame"` covers the whole chart.

`looks.rough()` leaves text as it is in either scope. A shader look with `scope: "frame"` runs over text too, so tick labels wobble or break into dots.

<Example
  code={roughFrame}
  title="The whole frame"
  blurb="scope: &quot;frame&quot; redraws the axis lines and ticks with the marks. fill: &quot;solid&quot; fills the dots."
  try={(
    <>
      <b>Drag</b> a dot. The line follows, redrawn.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="shaders" title="Shader looks">

A shader look runs over the chart's pixels once it is painted. It needs the canvas renderer, so set `renderer: new CanvasRenderer()`. Under SVG the chart draws without the look and logs a warning.

- `looks.sketch()` wobbles and doubles every line and gives fills a pencil grain. `amount` sets the wobble, from 0 to 2, and `grain` the grain, from 0 to 1.
- `looks.cartoon()` flattens colour into `levels` steps and inks an `outline` where colour changes, in the colour `ink`.
- `looks.halftone()` redraws the chart as dots `size` px apart, on a screen turned by `angle` degrees.

The built-in shaders move a pixel by less than 4px, so a mark is drawn where the pointer hits it.

<Section id="custom" title="Your own shader">

`looks.custom(fragment, options)` runs your GLSL. `fragment` is GLSL ES 1.00 source that defines `vec4 look(vec2 uv)`, which returns the pixel at `uv`. Colour is premultiplied by alpha, both in the frame you read and in the pixel you return.

A fragment can read and call:

<div className="api-table-wrap">
  <table className="api-table">
    <thead>
      <tr><th>Name</th><th>Type</th><th>What it is</th></tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td><code>u_frame</code></td><td><code>sampler2D</code></td><td>The painted chart.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><code>u_resolution</code></td><td><code>vec2</code></td><td>The frame's size in device px.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><code>u_pixelRatio</code></td><td><code>float</code></td><td>Device px per chart px.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><code>u_seed</code></td><td><code>float</code></td><td>The <code>seed</code> option.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><code>chartPx(uv)</code></td><td><code>vec2</code></td><td>A <code>uv</code> as chart px, whose origin is the top-left corner.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><code>toUv(px)</code></td><td><code>vec2</code></td><td>Chart px as a <code>uv</code>.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><code>hash(p)</code></td><td><code>float</code></td><td>Noise from 0 to 1 at a <code>vec2</code>, a new value at every point.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><code>noise(p)</code></td><td><code>float</code></td><td>Noise from 0 to 1 at a <code>vec2</code>, smooth between whole numbers.</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>

`uniforms` passes your own numbers under their own names: a number arrives as a `float`, and two to four numbers as a `vec2`, `vec3` or `vec4`.

A fragment that fails to compile is skipped, and the compiler's error is logged. A shader that moves pixels further than 4px draws a mark away from where the pointer hits it.

  try={(
    <>
      <b>Edit</b> <code>strength</code> or <code>tint</code> in the code.
    </>
  )}
/>

</Section>

<Section id="glyphs" title="Faces, emoji and glyphs">

A look covers a glyph part by part, and each part keeps its own edit. `looks.rough()` redraws shapes, so it reaches a [`face`](/marks/face) and a [`composite`](/marks/composite) glyph, such as the plant from the [glyph case study](/case-studies/glyphs). An emoji from a [`symbol`](/marks/symbol) channel is text, so `looks.rough()` leaves it as drawn and a shader look reaches it.

<Section id="limits" title="Limits">

- A shader look reads the chart's pixels. A chart that draws [`geoTile()`](/marks/geo) tiles from a server without CORS headers draws without its shader looks and logs a warning.
- A browser with no WebGL draws the chart without its shader looks and logs a warning.
- `looks.rough()` leaves text and images as drawn.

</Section>
